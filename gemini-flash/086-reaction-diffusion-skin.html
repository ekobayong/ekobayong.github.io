Method

The morphology on display is governed by Alan Turing’s morphogenetic paradigm, codified here via the Gray-Scott model. Two virtual substances diffuse through continuous substrate: substrate A replenishable across the boundary, and autocatalytic antagonist B which converts A into B while decaying.
