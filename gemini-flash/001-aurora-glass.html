PHILOSOPHY & FOUNDATIONS

Three ideas behind Lumen

Light fixtures shouldn't be binary switches on a wall. They should operate like living organs of your architecture, breathing in harmony with solar time.

01 / BIOLOGY

Circadian by default

Direct retinal synchronization. Morning sunlight spikes melanopsin-stimulating frequencies to awaken cognition, smoothly decaying into low-CCT amber and candle spectrums as dusk draws in.

02 / FLUIDITY

Scenes that blend

Zero jarring steps or sudden switch clicks. Every lighting transition unfolds across 45-minute continuous logarithmic curves, mirroring the imperceptible dissolution of daylight into astronomical night.

03 / PRIVACY

Local silent yours

Every calculation runs on a dedicated 2-watt neural controller plugged into your home distribution board. No internet dependency, zero outbound telemetry, and 100% operational autonomy.
