Design Principles

Three ideas behind Lumen.

Circadian

Circadian by default

Zero toggles. Lumen follows the physical solar curve, warming to firelight as evening arrives to protect melatonin synthesis naturally.

Transitions

Scenes that blend

Light should never snap. Shifts take minutes, not seconds. Changes are imperceptible to conscious vision while setting your subconscious at ease.

Architecture

Local, silent, yours

Runs on a fanless edge hub inside your walls. Never connects to an ad server, never relies on an external cloud, and operates completely offline.
