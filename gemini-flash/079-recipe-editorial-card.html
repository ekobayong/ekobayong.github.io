Saffron & Blood Orange Tart

Piedmontese cold-press olive oil pastry cradling an infusion of Persian Sargol saffron curd, cloaked in translucent pinwheels of ruby Moro orange.

Prep 45 min
Bake 35 min
Chilling 3 hrs
Difficulty Master
Ingredients
8

Sweet Olive Crust

Saffron Silk Curd

Citrus Mosaic

Method & Technique

  1. Bloom the Sargol Saffron

    Gently pulverize saffron threads with a pinch of caster sugar in an agate mortar. Steep in 30ml blood orange juice warmed to exactly 50°C. Rest to release essential oils.

    15:00

  2. Tender Olive Crust

    Whisk flour, icing sugar, and sea salt. Stream in cold-press olive oil and yolks until a crumb forms. Shape into a disc and chill thoroughly.

    30:00

  3. Blind Bake the Shell

    Roll to 3mm thickness, tuck into a 24cm fluted flan ring, dock with a fork, and bake weighted at 175°C until pale gold and fragrant.

    20:00

  4. Temper the Curd Base

    Whisk eggs, sugar, remaining citrus juice, and vibrant saffron infusion in a copper bowl over a gentle water bath until glossy and thick ribbons form.

  5. Emulsify Cold Butter

    Remove bowl from heat; cool to 55°C. Mount with chilled butter cubes one by one with an immersion blender to yield an opaque saffron velvet emulsion.

  6. Fill and Set Tart

    Pour the silken curd into the cooled fluted pastry shell. Smooth using an offset spatula. Transfer to refrigeration to allow the saffron curd to firm.

    120:00

  7. Mandoline Citrus Rounds

    Slice blood oranges wafer-thin (1.5mm) across their equatorial equator. Poach gently in reduced orange blossom honey glaze for 3 minutes until translucent.

    03:00

  8. Compose the Rosette

    Drain citrus slices on parchment. Arrange six rounds in a concentric flower pattern around a solitary central slice. Brush with warm honey glaze and scatter stray saffron threads.

Pairing Notes
  • Vin Santo del Chianti — Raisined honeyed stone fruits echo the saffron's depth.
  • Smoky Earl Grey — Bergamot oil mirrors and cuts the bittersweet orange zest.
  • Cartizze Prosecco — Fine minerality and lively effervescence balances olive oil richness.
Baker's Note on Saffron
Take care never to boil saffron threads; excessive heat fractures their delicate aroma into harsh medicinal bitterness. A lukewarm bloom preserves the true sweet honey-hay bouquet.
