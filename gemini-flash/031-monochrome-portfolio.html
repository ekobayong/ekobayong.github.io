02 — Practice

Philosophy

Design is not decoration. It is the discipline of leaving things out until only what matters remains — then giving that thing room to breathe.

Operating between Lisbon and Berlin, the studio develops comprehensive visual identities, editorial publications, and spatial systems for cultural institutions, architectural practices, and discerning commercial founders. Every engagement begins as a deliberate exercise in reduction: examining the structural essence of an idea, unburdening it of decorative surplus, and formulating a visual language that endures beyond ephemeral stylistic cycles.

Working collaboratively with curators, editors, and master craftspeople, the studio unites intellectual rigor with mechanical sensitivity. Digital interactions are architected with the tactile discipline of print; physical editions are produced in close partnership with archival binderies and letterpress workshops across Portugal and Germany.
