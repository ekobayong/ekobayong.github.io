1919–1933 · Fourteen years of form follows function

In 1923, Wassily Kandinsky circulated a questionnaire at the Bauhaus, asking respondents to pair the primary colors—red, yellow, and blue—with the primary geometric forms: triangle, square, and circle. The canonical synthesis remains a cornerstone of modernist constructivism.
