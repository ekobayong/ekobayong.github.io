The Greedy Resolver

A continuous tone is discretized into chord steps. From the currently anchored nail, the algorithm simulates cords extending to all other perimeter nails. Each path calculates line integrals across an inverted residual target map. The line that absorbs the greatest deficit is tensioned, and its footprint is subtracted. Step by step, overlapping paths converge upon half-tones with mathematical determinism.

Optical Tone & Shading

Human eyes perceive lines packed beneath resolving thresholds as continuous surface shading. Straight segments create subtle caustic curves and density gradients via moiré interference. Single semi-translucent passes produce high-key highlights, while multiple criss-crossing passes accumulate into dense, opaque shadows, mimicking an intaglio print.

Physical Workshop Craft

To materialize this artifact at scale, cut a 60 cm circular or square birch plywood base. Map radial perimeter angles using a drafting protractor or printed 1:1 vinyl template.
