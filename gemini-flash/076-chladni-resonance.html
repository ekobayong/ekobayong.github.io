The 1787 Experiment: In his seminal treatise Entdeckungen über die Theorie des Klanges, German physicist and musician Ernst Florens Friedrich Chladni demonstrated that drawing a violin bow across the edge of a sand-strewn brass plate produced symmetric nodal lines where the vibrating surface remained entirely at rest. Napoleon Bonaparte famously remarked, "Chladni made us see sound."
