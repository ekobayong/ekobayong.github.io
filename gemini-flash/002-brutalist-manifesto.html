HONEST DE-SIGN OR NOTHING

We reject the cowardice of gaussian blur elevation, hidden predatory defaults, and anemic pastel aesthetics. The user interface is not a theater stage; it is a physical instrument of human cognition. When an edge exists, mark it. When weight is present, declare it. Function demands honesty.

THE CODIFIED ARTICLES OF UNCOMPROMISING INTERFACES 9 CLAUSES
01

BORDERS ARE LEGAL CONTRACTS

An unbordered container is an act of epistemic deception. When you blur the boundary between a surface and its void, you conceal where interaction begins and where responsibility ceases. Every card, button, and viewport must be bounded by a 4px physical stroke. True structural hierarchy is established through ink, spatial rigor, and unapologetic geometry.

02

WEIGHT IS INFORMATION

Typography is not an ornamental veneer; it is structural kinetic mass. Heavy strokes command urgency; monospace grids enforce tabular integrity. When every element whispers in anemic featherweight pastels, nothing possesses authority. Make the primary voice heavy enough to leave an impression.

03

SHOW THE SEAMS

Never conceal the scaffolding that sustains computation. Screws, seams, gridlines, and execution traces are not flaws to be covered in frosted glass. Expose the structural logic. Software is an engineered apparatus, not an illusionist parlor trick.

04

ZERO DECORATIVE COWARDICE

If an embellishment does not instruct, protect, or compute, strip it down to the raw substrate. Rounded corners engineered to pacify consumer anxieties are concessions to sentimental design. 90-degree angles demand deliberate human engagement.

05

FRICTION IS PROTECTION

The pursuit of 'frictionless' engagement is an exploitative vector designed for impulse and extraction. Erasing data, parting with currency, and transferring sovereignty must encounter mechanical resistance. A button that alters state must require genuine intent and physical weight.

06

DATA OVER AIR

White space is valuable only when it clarifies relationship, never when used as theatrical emptiness to disguise intellectual vacancy. Respect human bandwidth. Pack high density with absolute typographic clarity.

07

REJECT THE GAUSSIAN LIE

Diffuse shadows mimic an imaginary overhead sun that does not exist above an emissive display panel. Hard 90-degree unblurred black offsets do not pretend to be atmospheric light; they are mathematical tokens of graphic displacement. They assert that an element has been lifted through shear, not suspended in ethereal heaven.

08

LATENCY IS A MORAL FAILURE

Four megabytes of script runtime to render static prose is ecological disrespect. Ship pure markup that paints before the user's retina can register hesitation. Respect time as an unrecoverable resource.

09

THE INTERFACE IS THE MACHINE ITSELF

There is no metaphysical essence lingering behind the glass. The glass is the terminal; the markup is the structure; the interaction is the covenant. When we engineer honest interfaces, we treat the user as an autonomous operator rather than captive attention. No deceptive dark patterns. No fake affordances. No compromise. Show the machine.
