An Intimate Sanctuary

Where limestone cliffs merge with open oceanic expanse.

Casa Maré was envisioned not as a hotel, but as a coastal estate suspended between salt and terra-cotta. Each space is intentionally understated, framing the dramatic Southern Portuguese horizon through whitewashed arches, raw linen, and native stone.

Rhythms of the Coast

Curated Moments

01 07:00 · Dawn

Sunrise Tide Swim

Follow our guide down private cliff steps into the calm sheltered bay as the morning mist clears.

02 10:30 · Morning

Rock-Pool Foraging

Explore low-tide marine geology, discovering samphire, wild sea herbs, and tidal fauna.
