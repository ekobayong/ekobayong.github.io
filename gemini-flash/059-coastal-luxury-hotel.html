The Sanctuary

Perched eighty meters above Praia da Luz, Casa Maré is an architectural ode to Atlantic stillness.

Conceived as a retreat for contemplation, the twelve ocean chambers merge raw Monchique granite, lime-washed plaster, and handwoven linens.

Here, time is measured not by hours, but by the slow arrival of the tides and the shifting amber warmth upon the cliff walls. No plastics touch our grounds; our kitchen sources exclusively from artisanal day-boats in Sagres and organic orchards across the Algarve foothills.

Curated Moments

Daily Rhythms of Praia da Luz

Intimate rituals crafted to synchronize your heartbeat with the tide.

01
06:30 • Dawn

Sunrise Cove Swim

Descend our private cliff stairway to Praia da Luz cove before the morning wind begins. Crisp Atlantic salt water followed by mountain sage tea on the warm limestone.

02
10:00 • Morning

Rock-Pool Walk

Forage wild samphire and examine tidal ecologies alongside our resident ocean biologist across the secluded intertidal shelves of Praia da Luz.

03
13:30 • Afternoon

Terrace Embers & Catch

Wild sea bass blistered over olivewood embers on our pergola terrace. Served with vine-ripened tomatoes, sourdough, and cold Bairrada natural wine.

04
16:30 • Golden Hour

Sail to the Caves

Board our restored 1968 wooden cutter to navigate the sea arches and submerged grottos of Ponta da Piedade as golden sunlight turns the cliffs amber.

05
21:30 • Nightfall

Stars from the Roof

Zero light pollution over the southern Atlantic. A brass telescope, handwoven wool throws, and aged 20-year Tawny Port beneath the celestial Milky Way.
