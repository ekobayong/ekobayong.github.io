ROTATED HALFTONE SCREENS

Color comic printing offsets screen lattices to suppress distracting moiré interference: Cyan at 0°, Magenta at 60°, Yellow at -15°, and Key (Black) at 30° relative to the base angle. Dot radii scale directly with ink density.

BEN-DAY PROCESS

Invented in 1879 by Benjamin Henry Day Jr., this reproduction method composites primary CMYK inks over unbleached pulp paper using optical color mixing to create vibrant secondary tones.

SOBEL COMIC INKING

Black graphic containment strokes are generated directly from the procedural scene luminance using a 3×3 Sobel edge-detection filter dilated with sub-pixel offsets for authentic pulp press registration.
