I / IX
Scroll
I
The harbor holds its breath against the dark. Oars rest. The pilings listen.
II
Night pours itself into every hollow of the tide and calls the boats by nothing.
III

Salt, then silence, then the long unscrolling of water remembering the moon.

Stay with the hush a little longer.

IV

We came here once

to name the black horizon

and failed, beautifully.

V
The keel of the night draws a single silver line from here to nowhere.
VI
Fish-lanterns, far out— or are they stars that learned to swim?
VII
Every wave is a letter the dark cannot keep and will not send.
VIII
If you listen past the surf you can hear the tide say almost your name.
IX
Morning will arrive late, and dripping light.
