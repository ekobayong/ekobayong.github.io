Method

Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion on a 200×200 torus. Chemical A diffuses quickly (DA=1.0) while B is slower (DB=0.5). Feed f replenishes A; kill k removes B. The 3×3 Laplacian uses centre −1, orthogonal 0.2, diagonal 0.05.

Handling

Paint B onto the glass with a continuous stamp so fast strokes remain unbroken. Metabolism sets steps per frame. Photograph exports a 1600×1600 plate with a caption in the corner. Pause freezes the culture without clearing it.

Provenance

Pattern names follow Pearson’s taxonomy of Gray–Scott morphologies — coral, mitosis, worms, spots, maze — as catalogued in the 1993 complex-systems survey of feed–kill space.
