How the screens work

Each channel is sampled on a rotated lattice — cyan at the base angle, magenta +60°, yellow −15°, black +30°. Dot radius grows with the square root of ink density so midtones stay open. A Sobel edge pass on luminance is dilated into comic outlines. Ben-Day fills are the same idea as the yellow page: a regular grid of dots that the eye blends into colour.
