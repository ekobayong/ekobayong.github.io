Three ideas behind Lumen

Rhythm

Circadian by default

Lamps follow a cosine of daylight. Warmth arrives at dusk without a switch, and cools again as the house sleeps.

Blend

Scenes that blend

Harbor dusk does not snap to reading amber. Colour and intensity crossfade so the room never notices the seam.

Quiet

Local silent yours

Schedules live on the hub. No cloud, no voice, no listening. Light that belongs to the walls that hold it.
