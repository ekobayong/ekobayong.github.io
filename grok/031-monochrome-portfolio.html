02 — Practice

About

Design is not decoration. It is the discipline of leaving things out until only what matters remains — then giving that thing room to breathe.

Ines Varga is an independent art director working between Lisbon and Berlin. She builds visual systems for museums, publishers and small manufacturers — work that is meant to last longer than a season.

The studio is small on purpose. Projects are taken one at a time so each identity can be reduced until the remaining form is inevitable.
