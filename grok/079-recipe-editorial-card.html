Saffron & Blood Orange Tart

A burnished saffron custard in a fluted pâte sucrée, finished with a mosaic of blood orange and three threads of the real spice.

Prep40 min
Bake35 min
Rest2 hr
DifficultyConsidered

Ingredients

8 servings

Pâte sucrée

    Saffron custard

      To finish

        Method

        1. Blitz flour, sugar and cold butter to sand. Beat in the egg until the dough just gathers. Chill 30 minutes. 30:00
        2. Roll the pastry, line a 23 cm tart tin, and freeze 10 minutes. 10:00
        3. Blind-bake at 180°C until the edges take colour, about 18 minutes. Remove weights and bake 6 minutes more.
        4. Warm cream with saffron threads until it blushes gold. Steep 15 minutes. 15:00
        5. Whisk yolks, sugar and zest. Stream in the saffron cream, then strain into the shell.
        6. Bake at 150°C until the custard just trembles, 22–28 minutes. 25:00
        7. Cool, then rest in the refrigerator until set. 2:00:00
        8. Arrange blood-orange slices in a tight mosaic, scatter three saffron threads, and serve at cool room temperature.
