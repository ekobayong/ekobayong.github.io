The greedy stitch

From the current nail every other pin beyond a minimum angular gap is scored by the mean residual sampled along the chord. The darkest unused pair is chosen, 0.13 of coverage is subtracted, and the weaver steps forward. It is not optimal — it is hungry, which is enough.

Why straight lines read as shade

A single hairline is almost nothing. Stacked at slight angles they densify like hatching. The eye integrates the crossings into tone, so a curve appears where no curve was drawn — ten thousand straight lines make a curve.

Building it in the shop

A 60 cm board, evenly spaced nails, and one continuous thread. Record the nail sequence from the loom, then wind in order. Tension is the whole craft: too slack and the image fogs; too tight and the frame sings.
