How it works

A white ray is a crowd of wavelengths. Glass slows each colour by a slightly different amount, so each bends by a slightly different angle at every surface:

Snell  n₁·sin θ₁ = n₂·sin θ₂

Cauchy n(λ) = A + B / λ²  ·  A = 1.5046, B = 0.0042 (λ in µm)

The 400–680 nm fan traced here uses that index at every refraction — violet, which glass slows most, is bent hardest and lands farthest from the beam axis.
