1919–1933 · Fourteen years of form follows function

In 1922, at the Bauhaus in Weimar, Wassily Kandinsky put a question to his students: which colour belongs to which shape? The majority answered as he did — yellow is an acute triangle, blue the deepest circle, red the square that neither moves nor feels. This poster machine takes the questionnaire seriously. Every composition begins from a number and argues its way across a 6 × 9 grid in three primaries and one black.
