Each painting begins from a six-digit seed. The seed selects a point in a two-octave noise field; the angle of the current at every drop of ink is drawn from it. Three passes follow — first the pale wide washes, then the mid strokes, and finally the fine hairs — each one dry-brushing its way toward the tip of the stroke, the way a real brush runs out of ink.
