Nocturne for the Sea

Scroll slowly. The poem keeps time with you.

I
Night unfastens the shore's bright stitching. Darkness leans its whole weight against the tide.
II
Far out, a ship keeps burning one small orange word into the water.
III
The horizon holds the distance it was given — neither nearer nor further than grief.
IV
Every wave bends at the same hinge and comes back the way the drowned do: all at once, softly.
V
Down where the light quits, the sea rehearses its darkness, filling every hollow until it can hold a moon.
VI
Then something surfaces: the salt memory of every harbor that ever trusted a lamp.
VII
Stars come loose from the water — the ones we dropped there as children, still blinking where they fell.
VIII
Listen — the whole coast is saying something almost your name.
IX
So keep your ledger, night. We were here. The sea remembers us dripping light.
