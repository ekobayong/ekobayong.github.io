About

Design is not decoration. It is the discipline of leaving things out until only what matters remains — then giving that thing room to breathe.

Ines Varga is an independent art director working between Lisbon and Berlin. For fifteen years she has built identities, publications and exhibitions for institutions that measure success in decades — a poetry press, a maritime museum, a watchmaker with ninety employees.

Before going independent she led the studio at a large European agency, where she learned that most brand problems are really editing problems. She teaches a yearly seminar on the printed page and keeps a standing offer to set one student's thesis in type for free.
