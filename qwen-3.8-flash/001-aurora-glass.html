Three ideas behind Lumen

Everything Lumen does grows out of three commitments about what light in a home is actually for.

Circadian

Circadian by default

Every scene is anchored to your sunrise and your bedtime — not a timer. Brightness and temperature drift together from 5,600 K noon to 1,900 K embers, so the house tells your clock the truth.

Blend

Scenes that blend

Light crosses thresholds the way sound does. Walk from kitchen to living room and the two scenes cross-fade over the doorway — no seams, no snapshots, no room that forgot what time it is.

Local

Local, silent, yours

Lumen runs entirely on the hub in your hallway. No cloud round-trips, no accounts listening in, no telemetry leaving the house. Your routines are the most intimate data you own — they stay home.
