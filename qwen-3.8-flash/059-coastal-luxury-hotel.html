The House

A small hotel that behaves like a home — one that happens to float above the sea

Casa Maré began as a fisherman's boathouse and a fig orchard. We kept the lime-washed walls and the old cistern, added twelve rooms, and let the ocean do the decorating. There is no lobby music, no queue at reception: your key is waiting, and the terrace door is usually open.

Mornings smell of coffee and salt. The cliff path down to Praia da Luz starts at the olive at the end of the garden. In the afternoon the house tilts into shade, and the only decisions are whether to swim, read, or do neither with great intention.

Experiences

The day, arranged by tide and light

01 06:50 · First light

Sunrise swim

Hot towels on the rail, coffee in a clay cup, and the flat silver sea before anyone else is awake.

02 09:30 · Low tide

Rock-pool walk

An hour along the shelf with Teresa, who counts octopus dens and names every anemone between here and Luz.

03 19:00 · Golden hour

Terrace grill

Whatever the boats and the garden gave that day, over vine embers, with a cold white that keeps sweating.

04 11:00 · Slack water

Sail to the caves

The old boat María Helena, out through the mouth of the gorge and into the blue light of the sea caves.

05 22:30 · After dark

Stars from the roof

Blankets, a bottle of Portage, and the landlord's telescope pointed — weather willing — at Saturn's rings.
