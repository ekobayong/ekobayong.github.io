Amenities

Everything a modern space-age motorist needs

The Kidney Pool

Heated, chlorinated, and shaped like the interior of a fascinating organ. Lifeguard on duty from ten until the stars come out.

Colour Television

In every room, tuned to exactly one channel: the west. The sun performs nightly, and the reception is outstanding.

Sputnik Lounge

Cocktails served under a rotating ceiling fixture that has never once been in sync with anything, and that is exactly the point.

Rooftop Observatory

A 10-inch reflector under a dome that rolls open like an eyelid. Thursday nights: Saturn, through the telescope, for free.

Rocket Wake-up

Your alarm is a small tin siren that goes T minus ten, nine, eight — and stops. Nobody has ever slept through it. Twice.

Covered Carport

Two spots per room, one of them with a painted rocket on the asphalt so you always park straight into the future.
