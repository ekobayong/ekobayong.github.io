Method

Two chemicals, A and B, diffuse at different rates and react: A feeds B, B kills A. The local balance tips, and the skin of B grows, spaces, or streaked — a coral reef, a lung, a lake. The f/k plane is a map of living ink.

Handling

Drag across the glass to seed B along the pointer; the brush leaves a temporary wound that the reaction repairs in its own pattern. Reseeding drops seven random discs and one central culture, then stands back.

Provenance

After Gray & Scott (1983), as catalogued by Pearson (1993): spots, stripes, and labyrinths from a two-variable reaction. The label numbers f and k exactly as the plate would have typed them, were this plate 19th-century brass.
