FormFollowsFunction?

A series of twelve winter lectures on the morality of the manufactured object — what a chair owes its sitter, and what a sign owes its reader.

Free and open to the public. The talks are held in German with whispered translation into English and silence.

27 OCT01 / 12
S. Berger — The Object Demands

Why the simplest objects are the most opinionated: an opening talk on chairs, cups, and the moral weight of handles.

10 NOV02 / 12
E. Ostwald — Legible Cities

Signage as civic speech: what a railway timetable and a town plan have in common, and where they part.

24 NOV03 / 12
J. Hensel — The Honest Grid

Twelve columns and a guilty conscience: a manager of a design school defends the baseline to its own staff.

08 DEC04 / 12
M. Vogt — Form Follows Money

A polemic with receipts: who pays for the white space, and what they expect from it.

12 JAN05 / 12
R. Steiner & A. Kolb — Roundtable

Two engineers, one typeface, no moderator: a conversation on the ethics of the visible.

26 JAN06 / 12
K. Maier — The Final Lecture

The closing argument, delivered in pencil, on a sheet of graph paper, as a form of protest.

Practical · questions after each talk
