03 — About

Practice

Design is not decoration. It is the discipline of leaving things out until only what matters remains — then giving that thing room to breathe.

Ines Varga is an independent art director working between Lisbon and Berlin. Over ten years she has built editorial systems, exhibition identities and record sleeves for clients who distrust noise, using little more than paper, ink and an argument about margins.

Recent work sits in the permanent collection of the Museu do Design, and in the margins of several books she would rather not name.
