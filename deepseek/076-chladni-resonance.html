Chladni Resonance

Sand on a singing plate — Ernst Chladni, 1787, at the bow of a fiddle.

A square metal plate vibrates; the sand flees the moving regions and settles on the still lines of the pattern. In 1787 Chladni brought a bow across the edge and drew these figures out of dust — the first acoustic spectroscopy the world had ever seen.
