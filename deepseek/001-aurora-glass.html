Three ideas behind Lumen

Each one is a small rebellion against the light switch.

Idea 01

Circadian by default

Every room follows the sun's own curve — cool in the morning, molten in the evening — unless you say otherwise. Your body clock becomes the house rules.

Idea 02

Scenes that blend

Scenes are not on/off presets — they crossfade slowly enough that you never catch the room changing. Only afterwards you notice it has become new.

Idea 03

Local silent yours

Everything computes on a chip in your wall box. No cloud, no accounts, no listening — Lumen learns quietly and keeps its opinions at home.
