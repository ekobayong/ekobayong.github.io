The house

Perched above the waves, tuned to the tides

Casa Maré began as a fisherman's cottage and became, over three renovations and one very long argument about the colour of shutters, a twelve-room hotel that refuses to hurry.

01
Twelve rooms only

Never a thirteenth, never a wing. The house holds exactly as much as it can welcome properly.

02
The sea at every window

Every room faces the Atlantic; the sun sets into the water, and the water keeps the receipt.

03
Slow by design

No TVs, no minibar clicks, no announcements. The loudest thing on the property is the tide.
