02 — Practice

About

Design is not decoration. It is the discipline of leaving things out until only what matters remains — then giving that thing room to breathe.

Ines Varga is an independent art director working between Lisbon and Berlin. After a decade leading identity and editorial programmes for publishers and cultural institutions, she now works directly with a small number of clients each year.

Her practice is anchored in the printed catalogue: rigorous grids, restrained monochrome palettes and typography treated as the primary image.
