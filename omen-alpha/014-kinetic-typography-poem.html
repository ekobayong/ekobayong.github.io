I
midnight pours a slow silver wine into the bay
II
the moon a pale coin spent on black water
III
waves repeat one word over and over
IV
far out, a ship carries its small room of light
V
the sea keeps every promise it never made
VI
and I stand where the sand forgets my feet
VII
your voice still folds itself into every wave
VIII
the water says almost your name.
IX
and the night goes home dripping light.
