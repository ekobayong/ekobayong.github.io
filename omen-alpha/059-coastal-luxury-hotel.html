The house

Twelve rooms, one long horizon.

Casa Maré stands where the Algarve cliffs begin to soften into sand. Built in 1948 as a skipper's summer house, it keeps its thick white walls, its cool tiled floors and its long supper table — now joined by a small kitchen garden, a salt pool cut into the rock, and nothing else that isn't necessary.
