Three ideas behind Lumen
Circadian

Circadian by default

The system follows the sun, not a timer. Colour shifts through the day on a slow cosine curve, so light always feels like a natural hour of the sky instead of a setting you forgot to change.

Scenes

Scenes that blend

Rooms cross-fade rather than switch. When dinner becomes a film, the transition happens in the walls and the ceiling, over minutes — the way daylight hands over to dusk.

Private

Local, silent, yours

Lumen runs on a small box inside the house. It never calls the cloud, never listens, never reports. When the internet goes down, your lights do not notice.
