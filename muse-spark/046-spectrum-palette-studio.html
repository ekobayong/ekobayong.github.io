How the harmonies work

Analogous gathers neighbours ±28° for calm, editorial systems. Complementary sets the base against its opposite for maximum vibration — use it sparingly, one for ink, one for spark. Triadic spaces three hues 120° apart for playful, balanced sets. Split-complementary softens the clash with the two flanks of the opposite. Monochrome stays on one hue and lets lightness do the talking.
