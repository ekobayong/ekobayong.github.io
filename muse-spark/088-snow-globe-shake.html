HOW TO HOLD WINTER
Drag the globe — fast strokes stir the snow and rock the sphere on its spring. Shake past 900 px/s for a blizzard. The key winds a music-box phrase and slowly turns the scene.

On a phone, Enable motion lets a real shake of the handset snow the globe — spikes above 13 m/s² count as shakes.

HOW THE SNOW WORKS
Gravity pulls each flake by its size; sine-noise turbulence scales with flurry energy, which decays over ~2.6 s so a full shake settles in about ten seconds. Walls reflect elastically inside the circle; flakes land on a depth-graded floor line and re-loft when energy spikes. The globe itself rides two damped springs — drag offset and wobble angle — while the music key spins the scene through a decaying perspective turn.
