Cathédrale du Nord · n° 081

Rosace du Nord

A Gothic rose window in twelve-fold stone tracery — hover each pane to read its story
Twelve spokes for the twelve apostles — move across the glass.

Season tints the sun · tracery sets the spokes · every pane is focusable

Winter pale · summer gold · evening amber — the motes follow the beam

Twelve-fold symmetry · antique glass · 90-second sun

STONE

Ashlar blocks carry three stacked strokes — dark base, mid body, thin highlight — so each mullion reads as chamfered stone.

GLASS

Fractal noise displaces every edge by five units; the same noise mottles the panes through an overlay blend.

LIGHT

The floor pool is the glass itself — translated, skewed −28°, squashed to a third, blurred and screen-blended opposite the sun.
