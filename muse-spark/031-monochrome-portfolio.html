    About

    Design is not decoration. It is the discipline of leaving things out until only what matters remains — then giving that thing room to breathe.

    Ines Varga is an independent art director working between Lisbon and Berlin since 2013. She leads identity, editorial and exhibition programmes for cultural institutions, publishers and quiet brands.

    Her studio takes on four commissions a year. Each engagement begins with an archive visit and ends with a printed specimen — no templates, no noise.
