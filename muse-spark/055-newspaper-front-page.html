LATE EDITION

Airship Meridian Spans Ocean in Sixty-One Hours

Commander Voss signals “all well” off the Lizard — city to hold holiday upon landing.
— By E. Halloran · Wireless to the Gazette —

At half past two yesterday morning, to the flare of searchlights and the cheering of ten thousand throats at Harbour Field, the great dirigible Meridian slipped her moorings and rose into a windless dark, bound for Croydon with fourteen souls and four tons of mail. Sixty-one hours later she was reported off the Cornish coast, her engines beating true, her silver envelope unstained by storm. Captain I. Voss, late of the polar service, declared by wireless that the crossing had been “a promenade above the weather.” The Harbour Board last evening voted a public holiday and a gold freedom of the port to the crew. Bunting is ordered for the towers, and Bettany’s Orchestra will play upon the landing green.

Late wires

Council Votes 20-Mile Limit

After a debate of three hours the Council fixed twenty miles as the town speed, the motorists protesting in vain.

Gold Record on the Exchange

Meridian Copper touched 7⅜ amid the heaviest trading of the year. See table below.

Symphony by Wireless

Last evening’s broadcast from the Grand Hall was heard as far as the lightship, tone “fine as silk.”

Sporting

Rovers Take the Cup 2–0

A crowd of nine thousand saw the Rovers defeat the Academicals before tea, Bell scoring both goals in the second half amid great waving of scarves.

Society

Mrs. Aldous Hart gives a musical evening Tuesday; Miss Vane sang “The Lost Chord” to much applause.

Grand Hotel Fire

Guests Led Down Ladders; None Lost

Fire broke out in the linen room of the Grand Hotel at dawn and was mastered by two engines. Guests in dressing-gowns watched from the square; the manager served coffee throughout.

Council

Speed, Lights & the Sea Wall

The debate on motor speeds divided the chamber. Alderman Pryce called the motor “a dragon ill-suited to a Christian street.” The sea-wall vote passes to committee.

Harbour

Record Tonnage Entered

September entries exceed all former marks. The Board orders a new crane and a coat of paint for the custom house. Tides: high 6:12, low 12:40.

Amusements

Palace — “The Sea Hawk.” Bijou — Vaudeville. Rink — Skating till ten.
