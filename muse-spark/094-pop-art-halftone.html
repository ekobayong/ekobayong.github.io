HOW IT WORKS — BEN-DAY FOREVER

The source is drawn in code, read back with getImageData, then re-screened as CMYK dots on rotated lattices. Dot radius follows the square root of ink density. A Sobel pass finds edges for fat comic outlines.

  1. Screens: C +60° / M +0° / Y −15° / K +30° off your base angle.
  2. Ben-Day: one batched path per channel, composited with multiply.
  3. Outlines: Sobel on luminance, dilated with sub-pixel offsets.
  4. Bubble: draggable burst or speech overlay, editable text.
