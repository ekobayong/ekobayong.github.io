Three ideas behind Lumen

01

Circadian by default

Your colour temperature tracks the sun without you asking. Bright and cool at noon, warm and low by bedtime — tuned to the room, not to a slider.

02

Scenes that blend

No harsh jumps. Every scene cross-fades over minutes, so the light in the room is always already where you were heading.

03

Local, silent, yours

Scenes live on your own hardware. No cloud round-trip, no microphone, no subscription — the schedule runs even when the internet naps.
